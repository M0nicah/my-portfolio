Data bites

How I think through a problem

Short write-ups on real data questions. They focus on the reasoning, not just the answer.

Bookkeeping · M-Pesa

Why do small sellers' profit numbers come out wrong?

Many sellers run the shop and the household from the same M-Pesa line. Most tools count every outgoing payment as a business expense, so groceries end up reducing "profit".

3000Owner drawings get their own account in Bookworths. They're equity, not an expense.

Retail · Profitability

Can a product bring in high revenue and still lose money?

Yes. A revenue-only view hides it. Add a profit rate, SUM(profit)/SUM(sales), by sub-category with a threshold flag and the problem products stand out.

Tables$207K in sales, −$17.7K in profit.

Marketing · ROI

Is the campaign with the best response rate the best campaign?

Campaign 4 had the highest acceptance (7.5%) and Campaign 2 the lowest (1.3%). Without cost, offer value or margin data, though, that only ranks response. It doesn't rank return on investment.

RuleAsk for cost data before moving budget.

Churn · Causation

Do electronic-check payments cause churn?

Electronic-check customers churn at 45.3%, but the dataset has no dates, satisfaction scores or support history. The payment method probably marks a type of customer rather than causing them to leave.

LabelCall it a descriptive pattern to investigate, not a cause.

SQL · Technique

When should you use a window function instead of GROUP BY?

GROUP BY collapses rows into a summary. A window function keeps every row while adding running totals, ranks or period-on-period change, e.g. LAG(revenue,1) OVER (PARTITION BY region ORDER BY month).

RuleIf you need the aggregate and the rows together, use a window function.

KPI design

How do you build KPIs that connect to business goals?

Start from the goal and work backwards: what tells us we're on track, what daily metric feeds that, and where does the data come from?

ChainGoal → outcome → leading indicator → operational metric → source.
